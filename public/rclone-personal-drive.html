rclone personal drive

A personal, single-user Google Drive client configuration. Not a public service.

What it is

rclone personal drive is the developer's own installation of rclone, an open-source command-line program, configured with a private Google OAuth client. It lets the developer mount and access their own Google Drive from their own computers.

Who can use it

Only its developer. There is no sign-up, no hosted service, no backend server, and no access to anyone else's Google data. The only account it ever touches is the one that signs in to authorize it.

What it does with Google data

It lists, reads, and writes files in the authorizing account's own Google Drive, transferring them directly between Google and the developer's own machines. Nothing is shared with third parties. Full details are in the privacy policy, including the Limited Use disclosure for the Google API Services User Data Policy.
